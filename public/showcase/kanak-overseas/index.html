About Kanak Overseas

Family-run since 2003, export-grade by discipline

Kanak Overseas started as a single procurement desk in Andhra Pradesh and has grown into a full-chain rice exporter — sourcing, milling, testing and shipping under one roof, so nothing is left to a middleman's judgment.

We work with importers who need a supplier that shows up the same way, shipment after shipment.
