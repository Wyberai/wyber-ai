# Paid-plan credits tripled (2026-11-09)

Decision: triple the credits on every paid plan at unchanged prices. The free plan stays at 30. Marketing angle: more credits for the same price.
(The wording "we moved to our own coding model" was NOT used anywhere in the code or copy: builds run on Sonnet 5.5.)

## The new table

| Plan | Price (monthly / annual) | Credits per month | Was | Price per credit (monthly) | Daily drip (unchanged) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | | 30 | 30 | | up to 30, capped |
| Spark (India) | ₹499 / ₹399 | 150 | 50 | ₹3.3 | 2 |
| Starter | $29 / $23 | 450 | 150 | $0.064 | 6 |
| Builder | $79 / $63 | 1,500 | 500 | $0.053 | 20 |
| Pro | $199 / $159 | 4,500 | 1,500 | $0.044 | 60 |
| Growth | $399 | 12,000 | 4,000 | $0.033 | 160 |
| Scale | $799 | 30,000 | 10,000 | $0.027 | 400 |

Top-up packs (same prices, product ids unchanged): $19 / ₹399 = 120 credits (was 40), $49 / ₹999 = 300 (was 100), $99 / ₹1,999 = 600 (was 200).

Why these:
- Spark is 3x its real 50 (the 100 in `PLAN_FACTS` was an unfinished offer that was never granted). It keeps India's Spark and its pack at the same rupees per credit (₹3.3), the parity the Dodo webhook comment calls for.
- Growth and Scale had to rise too: otherwise Growth (4,000 for $399) would be worse than Pro (4,500 for $199).
- The daily drip was left alone. Tripling it as well would push the cost of heavy use too high (see below).

## Cost check (one measured build: about $0.0113 of model spend per credit)

Worst case = the plan's credits AND daily drip fully used every month. Real use is lower.

| Plan | Credits at full use (monthly + 30 x daily) | Model cost at full use | Share of monthly price | Before the change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 630 | $7.1 | 24% | 13% |
| Builder | 2,100 | $23.7 | 30% | 16% |
| Pro | 6,300 | $71 | 36% | 19% |
| Spark | 210 | $2.4 | 40% | 21% |

Annual billing is 20% cheaper per month, so those shares are about a quarter higher. This is before payment fees, hosting, and unlogged model calls, and rests on ONE small build. Check it with `node scripts/ai-spend-report.mjs 7d` once the call logging is live.

## What changed in code
- `src/lib/plans.ts`: new credits, `TOPUP_PACKS`, `LARGE_PLAN_CREDITS`. It is the single source of truth.
- The Dodo webhook (what is actually granted), the MCP checkout, the pricing page, the dashboard usage bar and upgrade nudges now read from it instead of keeping their own numbers.
- Visible copy updated: pricing page and its translations, upgrade modal, emails, assistant prompts, credits and docs pages, blog posts, use-case and comparison pages.
- `src/lib/plan-credits.test.ts` pins the numbers and fails if any file states an old plan allotment.

## Not changed
- Prices, product ids, the Dodo catalog and environment variables (no change is needed there).
- Credit costs of actions (a small build is still 30 credits).
- The legacy Stripe webhook map (`pro` 250 / `business` 500), which uses other price ids and looks unused.
- Existing subscribers: they get the new amount at their next renewal; nothing is added mid-cycle.

## Rollback
`git revert` the commit. No data migration is involved; credits already granted stay granted.
