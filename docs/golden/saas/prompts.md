# Golden prompts: SaaS products
1. Appointment scheduling for salons: owners, staff, services, online booking page, reminders, a free and a paid plan.
2. Invoicing for freelancers: clients, invoices, payment status, reminders, subscription plans.
3. School fee collection: schools, classes, parents paying online, receipts, plans per school size.
4. Inventory for small retailers: stock, suppliers, purchase orders, low-stock alerts, team roles.
5. HR leave and attendance for small companies: employees, leave requests, approvals, plans per headcount.
6. Customer feedback collection: forms, responses, a simple dashboard, free and pro plans.
7. Time tracking and billing for agencies: projects, timesheets, invoices, roles.
8. Gym membership software: members, plans, renewals, check-ins, payments.
9. Rental management SaaS: landlords, properties, tenants, rent collection, statements.
10. Restaurant table booking: restaurants, tables, guests, confirmations, no-show tracking.
11. A lead CRM for property brokers: leads, follow-ups, site visits, team.
12. A helpdesk: tickets, assignees, SLA timers, canned replies, plans.
