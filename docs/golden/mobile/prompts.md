# Golden prompts: mobile apps
1. A habit tracker with streaks and reminders.
2. An expense splitter for flatmates.
3. A delivery rider app: today's orders, navigation link, mark delivered, earnings.
4. A workout log with sets, reps and weekly progress.
5. An order app for a kirana store: browse items, cart, pay on delivery, order history.
6. Attendance for a tuition class: batches, mark present, parent message.
7. A field-sales visit log: shops, visit notes, photos, next visit date.
8. A flashcard app for learning Gujarati.
9. A salon appointment booking app for customers.
10. A recipe saver with shopping list.
11. A parking spot finder with saved places.
12. An event check-in app: guest list, scan or search, count.
