# Golden prompts: web apps (plain phrasing; add a Hinglish and a long-brief version when scoring)
1. A billing app for my kirana store: items with price and stock, bills for customers, udhaar (credit given) per customer, today's sales.
2. An appointment manager for a dental clinic: patients, appointments by day, treatment notes, follow-up reminders.
3. A fee tracker for a school: students by class, monthly fees, who has paid, who is overdue, receipts.
4. A reorder list for a steel shop: items, current stock, minimum level, suppliers, what to reorder this week.
5. A CRM for a tuition class: enquiries, demo classes, admissions, batch fees.
6. A rental property manager: flats, tenants, rent due dates, payments, maintenance requests.
7. A gym membership tracker: members, plans, expiry dates, renewals, attendance.
8. Job cards for a garage: vehicles, work to do, parts used, status, final bill.
9. Orders for a tailor: customers, measurements, delivery dates, advance and balance.
10. A booking board for an event planner: events, vendors, payments, checklist per event.
11. A leave and attendance tracker for a 25-person company.
12. A project board for a small agency: clients, tasks, owners, due dates, hours.
