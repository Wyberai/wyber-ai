Why every build is cream and orange: `src/lib/unslop.ts` (rule US2) gives the model three starter colour ramps (graphite, warm, ink) with one concrete example each. The warm one is worded "paper, food, craft, people", which fits nearly every small-business brief, and is paired with "warm worlds suit amber, coral or ember orange". Four of four default builds in this run came out cream with a rust or wine accent. An explicit colour instruction in the brief is honoured (see below), so this is a defaults problem. Websites in plan mode have a separate 30-direction picker; apps and SaaS do not.

Long-brief test (845 words, 15 numbered requirements plus a look-and-feel paragraph, web app, charged 160 credits, about 6 minutes):

- Colour direction followed exactly: dark slate background (rgb 17,22,29), teal accent, no cream, beige, orange or brown, no emoji, no gradients.
- Present and working when tested: three roles with a "screen lock, not security" note and role-based navigation; every patient field with the exact option lists; consent blocks saving ("Consent must be ticked before the patient can be saved."); Day and Week views; 30, 45 and 60 minute slots; starts 08:00 to 19:45; double-booking blocked with "Dr. Meera Kulkarni is already booked from 09:00 to 09:45 with Demo Asha Kulkarni."; the five statuses; plans with sessions, days, price and physio; 12 exercises with a print handout; invoices FW/2026-27/0001 with visit or package, Cash, UPI, Card, partial payment and a GST at 18 percent checkbox that is off by default; WhatsApp reminder links on Today that are only shown for patients with a number; working Hindi toggle; CSV import on Patients; change history and export and delete on Privacy and data; 10 patients labelled demo; blank clinic settings (name, address, phone, GSTIN, footer).
- SOAP notes and the pain score exist in the code (`Patients.tsx`) but I did not reach them through the UI, so that item is unverified in use.
- Not verified: print styles, 375px layout, large-text mode, the reports figures with real data.
