# Meta ad plan: book meetings at Tech Expo Gujarat

Event: Tech Expo Gujarat, 27–29 Nov 2026, from 9:30 AM, Gujarat University Convention & Exhibition Centre, Ahmedabad (booth S23) (one listing source: echai.ventures. Confirm with the organiser).
Landing page: https://wyberai.com/gujaratexpo
Shared spine with the homepage: promise "Build any digital product. Ship it in minutes.", tagline "Describe it. Ship it.". Ad hook may use "Looking to upgrade your business?".
Goal: booked 15-minute booth meetings with people who can actually buy.

## Final audience

**Pay to reach owners. Let students come free.**

| | Who | Paid Meta? | Share of budget | Landing link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Owners of small businesses in Ahmedabad (manufacturing, trading, services, clinics, CA firms) | Yes | 65% | `?for=business` |
| B | Owners and founders of software and IT-services companies | Yes | 35% | `?for=software` |
| C | Students | No paid spend | 0% | `?for=student` |

Why:
- A 15-minute slot is the scarce thing. Three days, one booth, roughly 60 to 90 usable slots. Fill them with people who can buy.
- Owners are reachable on Meta by location plus job title. "Students who want to freelance" are not, and they show up at expos anyway.
- Students still get a page that speaks to them. Reach them with the LinkedIn post, college WhatsApp groups and Instagram, at no ad cost.
- Meta cannot tell IT owners from non-IT owners reliably, so the split is done by creative and by ad set, not by clever targeting.

## Campaign structure

- **1 campaign.** Objective: Leads. Conversion location: Website. Conversion event: **Lead** (the form fires it on success; the pixel is confirmed live on the page).
- **2 ad sets** (A and B above), 3 ads each: the 9:16 reel, the 4:5 image, the 9:16 image.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (Reels, Stories, Feed). Keep the 9:16 assets for Reels and Stories, 4:5 for Feed.
- **Schedule:** start 10 Nov, end the morning of 27 Nov. That is about 17 days, so the pixel has time to learn before the event.
- **Location:** Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar plus 25 km. Languages: English, Hindi, Gujarati.

## Targeting (starting points, confirm what Ads Manager offers today)

- **A. Business owners:** age 25 to 55. Job titles: Owner, Proprietor, Founder, Managing Director, Director. Behaviour: small business owners. Interests as hints only: small business, entrepreneurship, GST.
- **B. Software owners:** age 24 to 50. Job titles: Founder, Co-founder, CEO, CTO. Industry: IT and software services. Interests as hints only: software development, startups.
- Leave Advantage+ audience on and use the above as suggestions. Exclude people under 24 from both.

## Budget (needs your number)

Suggested starting point: about Rs 1,200 per day in total, roughly Rs 780 for A and Rs 420 for B, for 17 days, about Rs 20,000. Nothing is spent until you approve a figure.
- After about Rs 3,000 of spend, pause the worst ad in each ad set and put its budget on the best.
- If a booking request costs far more than a meeting is worth to you, change the creative before raising the budget.
- Treat these as working rules, not benchmarks. I have no verified cost-per-lead figure for this audience.

## Before you launch

1. **Booth S23 is in the page, the creatives and the booking alert.** Keep it on any new asset.
2. **Ad account.** I do not have an Ads Manager token (only the pixel id), so you create the campaign in Ads Manager, or give me an API token and I will create it paused for you to review.
3. **Reply fast.** Everyone who books gets a WhatsApp from you within the hour. Slots go cold otherwise.
4. **Meta review.** First review usually takes under a day. Upload a few days before the start date.
5. **Policy.** The text on the images is short and nothing makes a claim about the viewer. If Meta flags an ad, soften the headline first.
