■ WHAT PEOPLE BUILD

Software for how India actually works.

Nine builds, three for each kind of visitor. Each starts with paperwork, a rule or a habit you will recognise, and a sentence you could say out loud.

LINE 1If you run a software company
WEB APP
Timesheet to invoice
Month-end. Hours are scattered across five sheets, and the US client wants the invoice by the 1st.
YOU GETHours per developer per clientInvoice in USD with the rupee valueWhich invoices are still unpaid
SAY THIS

Build a timesheet app where developers log hours per client project, and it creates a monthly invoice in USD with the rupee value and a paid or unpaid status.

WEB APP
Change-request log
The client asks for a "small change" on WhatsApp. Then another. None of it was ever priced.
YOU GETEvery request loggedHours and price estimatedClient approval before you start
SAY THIS

Build a change-request tracker where I log each client request with estimated hours and price, the client approves it, and I mark it billed.

PROTOTYPE
Pitch prototype, on the call
A pharma stockist explains how he takes orders over WhatsApp. You send a link to a working version before the call ends.
YOU GETA clickable prototypeReal code you can keepA reason for him to pick you
SAY THIS

Build a prototype for a pharma stockist to take retailer orders and track medicine expiry dates, with a login and an order status screen.

LINE 2If you run a business
WEB APP
Job-work challan tracker
You send goods to job workers every day, and nobody can say what has come back and what is still out.
YOU GETA record for every challanPending at each job workerA warning before the one-year return limit
SAY THIS

Build a job-work tracker where I log challans for goods sent to job workers, record what comes back, and get a warning before the one-year return limit.

WEB APP
GSTR-2B reconciliation
The 11th of the month. Your accountant matches the purchase register to GSTR-2B line by line, and three supplier invoices are missing.
YOU GETUpload both filesMatches by supplier GSTIN and invoice numberA list of suppliers to chase
SAY THIS

Build a tool where I upload my purchase register and my GSTR-2B file, match them by supplier GSTIN and invoice number, and show mismatches and a list of suppliers to chase.

WEB APP
Cheque and collections register
Post-dated cheques sit in a drawer, and you only find out one bounced when the bank calls.
YOU GETCheques by party and dateDeposit-day remindersBounced cheques tracked
SAY THIS

Build a cheque register where I log post-dated cheques by party, get a reminder on each deposit date, and track which ones bounced and what is still owed.

Scenes are illustrative. Build each one in WyberAi before the expo so every example is a real, openable demo at the booth. Check any compliance details (GST, job-work limits, cheques) with a CA before presenting them as advice.
