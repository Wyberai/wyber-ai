## 4. Replies to the questions you'll get

**Can it replace Slack, Teams or Salesforce?**
No, and the tool says so. Platforms like these come back as "not a one-to-one replacement". If your team uses one or two jobs of a big tool, we can build a focused version of just those.

**What does it cost?**
A typical build is about 160 credits, and you see the estimate before it starts. Changes afterwards are billed as normal edits. Outside services (email, SMS, AI) are billed by the provider to your own account.

**Is my data with you?**
No. The first build starts with sample data. To go live you connect your own free Supabase account, so the data is stored with you.

**Do you use my API keys?**
Your keys go in your own project settings and are passed to your live app. Calls to email, SMS or AI providers run on your own accounts, never ours.

**How is this different from other AI app builders?**
Most say they can build anything. We start from the tool you're replacing, and we mark what we can't build before you pay.

**Does it work for [tool]?**
Paste the URL and find out. 44 popular tools are already analyzed, and anything else is read live in about 15 seconds.
